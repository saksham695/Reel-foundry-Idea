# Brief — chai-mehngai

**Persona:** Rozana Hisaab — observational Hindi comedy about the arithmetic of
everyday Indian life. Dry, warm, never mean. The narrator is a friend who noticed
something, not a comedian doing a bit.

**Language:** Hindi (Devanagari), spoken register, Hinglish loanwords where a real
person would use them.

**Topic:** चाय की कीमत — how a ₹10 cutting chai got to ₹20 while everyone pretended
not to notice. The joke is in the arithmetic, not in a punchline at the end.

**Angle:** Count it out loud. Small, concrete, checkable numbers. End on a turn that
reframes rather than complains.

**Do not:** blame any government, party or community. No politics. No "aaj kal ke log".

**Format:** 6 beats, 35-45 seconds. Visual cues must be things a stock camera has
actually filmed — tea glasses, a roadside stall, coins on a counter, steam, hands.
