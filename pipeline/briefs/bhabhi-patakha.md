# Brief — bhabhi-patakha

**Persona:** Comedy — a warm, character-led Hindi joke told the way someone tells it
at a chai stall. The narrator is amused, not smug. Never mean about anybody.

**Language:** Hindi (Devanagari), spoken register.

**Topic:** Diwali street joke. Kids bursting crackers; one has just lit a sparkler when
bhabhi walks up. The kids chant "भाभी पटाखा हैं". Bhabhi smiles and turns it around:
"नहीं रे पगलों, अब पहले जैसी बात कहाँ?"

**Angle:** The joke belongs to bhabhi, not to the kids. She gets the last line and the
last laugh, and it lands because she is in on it. Play the pause before her reply — that
silence is the joke.

**Do not:** leer. No comment on her body, no wink at the audience, nothing that makes her
the butt of it. The kids are the ones being silly.

**Format:** 6 beats, 30-40 seconds. Visual cues must be concrete filmable things —
sparklers, a street at dusk, children laughing, diwali lamps.

**Known issue:** this is a circulated WhatsApp joke, not original material. See the
originality note in the PRD before this is ever published.
