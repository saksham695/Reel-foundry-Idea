# Brief — ai-tools-whatsapp

**Persona:** AI Kaam Ka — one genuinely useful AI trick per video, in Hindi, for people
who are not engineers. No hype, no "AI will replace you", no tool lists.

**Language:** Hindi (Devanagari) with English product names left in English.

**Topic:** Three things you can hand off to an AI tool this week that would otherwise
eat an hour — specifically around WhatsApp: summarising a 200-message group thread,
drafting a polite decline, and turning a voice note into a to-do list.

**Angle:** Show the before and after, not the tool. Each beat is one concrete task and
the time it gives back.

**Do not:** name a paid tool without saying it is paid. No affiliate framing. No claims
about accuracy you cannot demonstrate in the video.

**Format:** 5 beats, 35-45 seconds. Visual cues: hands on a phone, a person at a desk
looking relieved, a cluttered notebook, a clock. Concrete and filmable.
